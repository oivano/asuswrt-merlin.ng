##  ion Asus Merlin changelog  ##

Date: July 11, 2026
Release: 386.15-ion

  - CHANGED: Firmware tree streamlined for DSL-AC68U-only maintenance;
             unsupported model-specific prebuilt content was removed.
  - ADDED:   FRR WebUI support is now fully functional (backend handlers,
             route status, startup/restart flow, and proper stop-time route cleanup).
  - UPDATED: FRR stack refreshed (stable 7.5 line with newer libyang),
             with additional startup sequencing for PPP WAN environments.
  - ADDED:   OpenConnect VPN client support (dual-client UI, service control,
             status integration, and protocol support for AnyConnect/Juniper/
             Pulse/GlobalProtect/F5/Fortinet).
  - CHANGED: Legacy VPN surface reduced by removing PPTP/L2TP paths and
             modernizing IPsec handling.
  - FIXED:   Route-based IPsec reliability improvements (table 220 routing,
             LAN route correction, rp_filter tuning, and CTF bypass handling).
  - UPDATED: curl to 8.17.0.     
  - UPDATED: dnsmasq to 2.93.
  - UPDATED: OpenVPN to 2.7.5.
  - UPDATED: dropbear to 2026.92.
  - UPDATED: miniupnpd to 2.3.10.
  - UPDATED: haveged to 1.9.22.
  - FIXED:   Batch cherry-picks from upstream Merlin with severity-based inclusion;
             includes relevant security/high-impact fixes (notably OpenSSL backports)
             plus selected stability and compatibility fixes.
  - CHANGED: BWDPI dependency removed from Traffic Analyzer visibility;
             bwdpi was disabled in this branch.
  - FIXED:   Multiple WebUI/service quality fixes (Connections/Traffic Monitor,
             VPN status pages, WISP NTP display, AP-mode aaews crashloop,
             UPnP rule handling, and DHCP event ordering).
